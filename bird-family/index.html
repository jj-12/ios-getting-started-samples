What happened

Nathan and Lindsey Bird were raising six children in Riverdale, Utah. Lindsey is a teacher in the Weber School District. In the early hours of Thursday, October 1, 2026, a fire started near the front door of their home. Investigators later traced it to an overheated power strip. It spread quickly through the house, and the family was trapped inside.

Nathan and three of the children lost their lives that morning. A fourth child was taken to the hospital in critical condition and passed away a few days later. Lindsey and two of their sons survived. One of the boys was seriously burned and is still receiving care.

Those close to the family say Nathan got Lindsey and one of the children safely outside, then went back in for the others.

The family lost a husband and father, four children, their home, and nearly everything they owned. Hundreds of neighbors, students, colleagues and friends gathered for a vigil in the days that followed. Now the community is helping Lindsey and her sons with what comes next: laying their loved ones to rest, healing, and finding a place to call home.
