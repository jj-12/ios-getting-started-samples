After the fire of October 1, 2026

In a single night, the Bird family lost their home, their husband and father Nathan, and four of their children. Lindsey and two of their sons survived, one with serious injuries. They now face funeral and memorial costs, medical bills, and the long work of starting over. This page is where friends, neighbors and strangers can help.

Ways to help

Donations will help with funeral and memorial expenses for Nathan and the four children, medical care for the boys, and the urgent costs of starting over without a home. Lindsey and her sons are relying on this support. Every gift, of any size, helps. The family has asked that donations go only through the channels listed here so they can be sure the money reaches them.
