Support the Bird Family

After the fire of October 1, 2026

In a single night, the Bird family lost their home, their husband and father Nathan, and four of their children. Lindsey and two of their sons survived, one with serious injuries. They now face funeral and memorial costs, medical bills, and the long work of starting over.

Ways to help

Every gift, of any size, goes to Lindsey and her sons. The family has asked that donations come only through the official channels below so they can be sure the money reaches them.

  • Housing and the essentials of starting over
