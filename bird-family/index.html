Their story

In the early hours of Thursday, October 1, 2026, a fire broke out in the Bird family's home in Riverdale, Utah. Investigators later determined it was started by an overheated power strip near the front door. Nathan “Nate” Bird and three of his children died that morning. A fourth child, who had fought for days in the hospital, passed away a few days later.

Lindsey, a teacher in the Weber School District, survived along with two of her sons, one of whom was seriously injured. They lost their husband and father, four siblings, their home, and nearly everything in it, all in a single night.

Those who knew Nate describe a devoted husband and father. When the fire broke out, he got Lindsey and one of the children safely outside, and then went back in for the others. He never came out.

Hundreds of neighbors, students, colleagues and friends gathered for a vigil in the days that followed. This site exists so that the circle of people holding this family up can keep growing.
